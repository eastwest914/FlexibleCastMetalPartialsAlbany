Frequently Asked Questions

A partial denture replaces one or more missing teeth while your remaining natural teeth stay in place. It is removable and made to match the color and shape of your smile.

Flexible partials are lightweight and metal-free, while cast metal partials use a thin metal framework for added strength. The right choice depends on your teeth, bite and goals, and we review this during your evaluation.

Many repairs, such as cracks, broken teeth and loose fit, can be handled quickly, sometimes the same day. Call us at (518) 362-5987 and we will tell you what is possible.
